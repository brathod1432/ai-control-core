# Image Style Guide

## Size

Use 1080x1080 square images.

## Style

Enterprise, clean, technical, modern, calm, and professional.

## Colors

Dark blue, white, cyan, and grey.

## Recommended Visual Types

Flowcharts, dashboards, QA pipelines, AI monitoring layers, human engineer plus AI dashboard, CI/CD pipeline, risk matrix, and before/after comparison.

## Text Limit

Maximum 5 to 8 words on the image.

## Avoid

Too much text, company logos, fake confidential dashboards, random robot images, overly cartoonish AI brains, misleading claims, internal screenshots, and customer data.
