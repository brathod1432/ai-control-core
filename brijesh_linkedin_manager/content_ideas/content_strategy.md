# LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Positioning

Brijesh's position is practical AI for QA, enterprise software quality, test automation, release confidence, Scrum, Product Owner workflows, and engineering leadership.

## Main Content Pillars

* AI in QA
* Enterprise storage quality
* Test automation and CI/CD
* Release quality and risk management
* Scrum and Product Owner workflows
* Beginner-friendly AI education
* Personal brand and QA leadership

## Target Audience

QA engineers, automation engineers, software engineers, Scrum Masters, Product Owners, engineering managers, AI-curious professionals, enterprise infrastructure professionals, and recruiters.

## Tone of Voice

Simple, human, professional, practical, calm, and public-safe.

## Focus Topics

AI-assisted QA, test planning, log analysis, defect triage, flaky tests, CI/CD quality, serviceability, security testing, performance testing, release readiness, backlog decisions, sprint planning, and engineering judgment.

## Avoid

Internal company information, confidential product details, unreleased roadmap items, internal screenshots, customer data, unsupported vendor comparisons, and claims that AI replaces engineers.

## Balance

Use a 70/30 mix: 70 percent practical technical leadership content and 30 percent beginner-friendly AI education.

## Growth Through Engagement

Publish 3 times per week. Comment thoughtfully on 15 to 25 relevant posts per week. Reply to every meaningful comment. Track topics that create real conversations.
